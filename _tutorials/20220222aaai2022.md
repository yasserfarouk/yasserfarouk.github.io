- **Presenters**: [Yasser Mohammad](https://yasserfarouk.github.io){:target="_blank"} (NEC Corporation) and Amy Greenwald (Brown University).
- **Materials**: [Aaai2022AutomatedNegotiation on GitHub](https://github.com/yasserfarouk/Aaai2022AutomatedNegotiation){:target="_blank"} — slides, [handouts](https://github.com/yasserfarouk/Aaai2022AutomatedNegotiation/raw/main/handouts.pdf){:target="_blank"}, and hands-on notebooks.

#### Overview
This tutorial provides an overview of automated negotiation between intelligent agents: established results, the main open challenges, and the research platforms used to tackle them (including Genius and [NegMAS](https://github.com/yasserfarouk/negmas){:target="_blank"}). It combines conceptual segments with hands-on notebook demos for building and evaluating negotiating agents.
